<p class="eyebrow">A little about me</p>
<div class="prose about-profile">
<header class="page-heading"><h1>Hi, I'm Adele</h1> <p class="intro">(she/her), a <a href="https://www.ala.org/educationcareers/libcareers/jobs/reference" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Research</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Readers%27_advisory" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Readers’ Advisory</a> Librarian at <a href="https://www.richlandlibrary.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Richland Library</a> in Columbia, South Carolina. I help people find books they’ll love, get comfortable with technology, and find information they can trust. I like a good question, whether it’s about evaluating a source or finding a novel about a woman making spectacularly bad decisions <a href="https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/32871394-the-pisces" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">while falling in lust with a merman</a>.</p></header>
<p>At work, I co-facilitate <a href="https://www.richlandlibrary.com/events?keyword=The%20Melting%20Plot" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Melting Plot</a>, a book group centered on authors of color, and work to bring underrepresented voices onto our shelves. I teach people how to use Android and Apple mobile devices, get work done in Word and Excel, build a website with Wix, or use LLMs like ChatGPT, and I coordinate and schedule all computer classes and <a href="https://www.richlandlibrary.com/events?keyword=Computer%20Basics&amp;location[]=2792&amp;type[]=190" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Computer Basics Drop-Ins</a> at the main library. My research support work has included helping graduate students develop research questions, evaluate sources, and learn to manage citations with Zotero and Mendeley. On the staff support side, I'm a member of the library's <a href="https://www.richlandlibrary.com/tag/lgbt" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Rainbow Committee</a>, which facilitates LGBTQIA2S+ programming for the library and throughout our community.</p>
<p>In everything I do, I want people to feel that their interests matter and their questions are welcome. Learning something new can be uncomfortable. Asking for help shouldn’t make it worse. I’m particularly interested in accessible instruction and how libraries can better support neurodivergent learners.</p>
<p>I earned my <a href="https://sc.edu/study/colleges_schools/cic/academic_programs/masters/master_of_library_and_information_science/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">MLIS</a> at the <a href="https://sc.edu" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">University of South Carolina</a>, participated in The Network of the National Library of Medicine's <a href="https://www.nnlm.gov/offices/ncds/internship" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">National Center for Data Services</a> internship, and have worked on data analysis for my department and Richland Library's <a href="https://www.readfreelyfest.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Read Freely Fest</a>. I enjoy using surveys and evaluations to find out what’s working, and what we need to rethink.</p>
