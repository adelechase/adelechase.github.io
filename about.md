<header class="page-heading"><p class="eyebrow">A little about me</p><h1>Hi, I’m Adele.</h1></header>
<div class="prose" markdown="1">
I’m Adele Chase (she/her), a [Research](https://www.ala.org/educationcareers/libcareers/jobs/reference) and [Readers’ Advisory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Readers%27_advisory) Librarian at [Richland Library](https://www.richlandlibrary.com) in Columbia, South Carolina. I help people find books they’ll love, get comfortable with technology, and find information they can trust. I like a good question, whether it’s about evaluating a source or finding a novel about a woman making spectacularly bad decisions while falling in lust with a merman.

I co-facilitate [The Melting Plot](https://www.richlandlibrary.com/events?keyword=The%20Melting%20Plot), a book group centered on authors of color, and work to bring underrepresented voices onto our shelves. I teach people how to use Android and Apple mobile devices, get work done in Word and Excel, build a website with Wix, or use LLMs like ChatGPT, and I coordinate and schedule all computer classes at the main library. My research support work has included helping graduate students develop research questions, evaluate sources, and learn to manage citations with Zotero and Mendeley. On the staff support side, I'm a member of the library's Rainbow Committee, which facilitates LGBTQIA2S+ programming for the library and throughout our community.

Across my work, I want people to feel that their interests matter and their questions are welcome. Learning something new can be uncomfortable. Asking for help shouldn’t make it worse. I’m particularly interested in accessible instruction and how libraries can better support neurodivergent learners.

I earned my MLIS at the University of South Carolina, participated in the National Center for Data Services internship, and have worked on data analysis for my department and Richland Library's Read Freely Fest. I enjoy using surveys and evaluations to find out what’s working, and what we need to rethink.

Outside the library, I make pottery, love *Star Trek*, nurse a wicked podcast addiction, enjoy English-language stand-up comedy from around the globe, and follow enough international versions of *The Traitors* to have opinions across several time zones.
