<header class="page-heading"><p class="eyebrow">A little about me</p><h1>Hi, I’m Adele.</h1></header>
<div class="prose" markdown="1">
I’m a librarian at Richland Library in Columbia, South Carolina, and I earned my MLIS at the University of South Carolina.

Readers’ advisory is a big part of my work. I enjoy helping people find books that speak to their interests, including books they might not have found on their own.

I’m also interested in how libraries can make their services more accessible. My graduate research focused on autistic postsecondary students’ perceptions of sensory spaces in academic libraries.

Technology is another part of that work. I’ve taught classes in Excel and Microsoft 365, and I’ve worked on tools to make everyday library tasks easier.

Outside of work, I’m a reader and a Star Trek fan.
