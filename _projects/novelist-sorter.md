## The question

A long list of search results can be difficult to compare. I wanted a way to bring a NoveList search into one table and sort it around the information I needed.

## The project

The bookmarklet collects search results into a sortable table with titles, authors, Goodreads ratings, publication dates, review links, synopses, and subjects. Book covers appear below the titles.

## Why it matters

The table gives me another way to explore a search when I’m working on readers’ advisory or a book list.
