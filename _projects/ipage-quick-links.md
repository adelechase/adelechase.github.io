## The question

When I’m evaluating a book in iPage, I often want to look at information on Goodreads and NetGalley too. Switching between those pages adds extra steps to the process.

## The project

iPage Quick Links is a Firefox extension that brings Goodreads ratings and activity counts into an individual iPage product page. It also opens Goodreads and NetGalley pages so the book information can be reviewed directly.

## Why it matters

The goal is to make the lookup process easier while keeping the source pages available for review. The information supports a librarian’s judgment about a book.
