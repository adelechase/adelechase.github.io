## The question

How do autistic postsecondary students experience sensory spaces in academic libraries? How well do those spaces meet the needs of the people who use them?

## The proposal

My graduate research proposal explored a qualitative Photovoice approach, using participants’ images and discussion to understand their experiences. The proposal drew on the social model of disability, Universal Design for Learning, and critical disability studies.

## Why it matters

Designing an accessible space involves understanding how people experience it. This proposal placed students’ perspectives at the center of that question.

The full academic context and supporting coursework are in my MLIS portfolio.
