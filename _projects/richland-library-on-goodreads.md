## The question

When I come across a book on Goodreads, I want to know whether I can borrow it through Richland Library. Searching the library catalog, Libby, and Hoopla separately adds several steps to that process.

## The project

Richland Library on Goodreads is a Firefox extension that brings library availability information directly into Goodreads. It checks physical holdings in Richland Library’s catalog, live Libby availability, and Hoopla eBook and eAudiobook results. The extension uses the book’s title, author, and ISBN when available to search across the services.

## Why it matters

The extension makes it easier to move from discovering a book to borrowing it. By showing print and digital options in one place, it reduces repetitive searches and helps readers find a format that works for them.
