Contact

Adele Chase is a potter based in Columbia, South Carolina, with a particular fondness for the moment when a lump of clay starts looking as though it has plans of its own. Much of the making happens at 2 Story Studios in Cayce, amid the spinning wheels, glaze experiments, and considerable suspense of waiting for a kiln to cool. Her work occasionally leaves the studio and ventures into the world: select pieces are available for purchase at 2 Story or State of the Art Gallery and Pottery Studio. If something catches your eye, or you simply want to talk shop about handmade things and the strange alchemy of fire and mud, she’d be glad to hear from you. Reach her at adelechase@gmail.com, or follow her on Instagram at AuntAdelePicard to see what she’s working on before, and sometimes after, the kiln has the final say.
